Serving the community

What happens at the mosque

  • الصلوات الخمس

    Five daily prayers

    Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha are prayed in congregation throughout the year.

  • الجمعة

    Jumu‘ah

    The Friday khutbah and prayer, held every week of the year.

  • رمضان

    Ramadan & iftar

    Congregational prayers through the blessed month, and a free iftar served every day.

  • العيد

    Eid celebrations

    The community comes together to celebrate Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha.

  • صلاة الجنازة

    Funeral prayers

    Janazah prayers and memorial gatherings. When a loved one passes away, please contact us so arrangements can be made.

  • الإرشاد

    Spiritual counselling

    Guidance and support for anyone who needs someone to talk to about matters of faith and life.

The blessed month

Ramadan at Golborne Mosque

Throughout Ramadan the mosque holds congregational prayers and serves a free iftar every day of the month.

Getting here

  • Westbourne Park: Circle and Hammersmith & City lines, about 10 minutes’ walk.
  • Ladbroke Grove: Circle and Hammersmith & City lines, about 10 minutes’ walk.
  • Golborne Road runs between Portobello Road and Kensal Road, close to Trellick Tower.

When you visit

  • Please remove your shoes before entering the prayer hall.
  • Dress modestly. It is customary for women to cover their hair.
  • Please put phones on silent during prayer.
  • Visiting for the first time, or bringing a group? Call ahead and we will be glad to welcome you.
