Golborne Mosque · A3 poster footer (pattern only)
=================================================

The mosque's zellige pattern on the website's dark grey, with nothing else on
it, for the bottom of an A3 portrait poster. A gold edge runs along the top,
the mirror of the header's edge (see ../a3-poster-header and preview-on-a3.png).

Two heights
-----------
golborne-a3-footer        297 × 40 mm, a slim footer
golborne-a3-footer-tall   297 × 100 mm, the same height as the header

Which file to use
-----------------
*-print.pdf   For a printer or a design program. The size above plus 3 mm
              bleed on every side; the pattern is a 400 dpi picture and the
              gold edge is vector.
*.png         For Word, Canva, Publisher or Google Docs. Exactly the size
              above at 300 dpi, no bleed. Place it at the very bottom of an
              A3 page, full width.
*.svg         The editable source (with bleed).

The pattern lines up with the header's: a star sits on the poster's centre
line in both.
