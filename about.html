Our story

Forty years on Golborne Road

The story of Golborne Mosque

Golborne Mosque has been part of Golborne Road for forty years. Its story belongs to the Moroccan families who made this corner of North Kensington their home, and who worked, step by step, to give their community a place to pray.

  1. 1960s

    From northern Morocco to North Kensington

    The first Moroccans to settle in Britain came in the 1960s, most from the Jbala region of northern Morocco: Larache, Tetouan, Tangier and the villages around them. Many found work in London’s hotels and kitchens and made their homes in North Kensington, and from the early 1970s their families followed. What they did not yet have was a mosque of their own.

  2. 1981

    Three founders

    The mosque was founded in 1981 by three Moroccans, and most of its worshippers are of Moroccan origin.

  3. 1985–1987

    Looking for a home

    In 1985 the community applied to turn 76 Golborne Road, then a shop, into a mosque. The council refused twice, and an appeal was dismissed in 1987. In the meantime, on 3 July 1986, the Golborne Mosque Trust was registered as a charity.

  4. 1989

    Permission at last

    In February 1989 the council gave permission for a mosque in the basement of number 76. After four years of trying, the mosque had a home on Golborne Road.

  5. 2000s

    Little Morocco

    By now Golborne Road was known as “Little Morocco”, lined with Moroccan cafés, butchers and grocers. A 2009 government study named the mosque as one of the two most used by Britain’s Moroccan community, especially by older worshippers, who gathered to recite the hizb of the Qur’an together.

  6. Today

    Still part of the community

    The Golborne Mosque Trust still looks after the mosque. It holds the five daily prayers and Jumu‘ah, a free iftar every day of Ramadan, the Eid prayers, funeral prayers and spiritual counselling. From the dawn prayer to the last prayer of the night, it remains part of the daily life of Golborne Road.

About Golborne Mosque Trust

Golborne Mosque Trust is an established Islamic charitable organisation serving worshippers and the wider community in the Golborne Road area of London W10.

At the heart of our mission is the provision of a welcoming place for daily prayers, Friday congregational prayers, and community engagement.

We aspire to be much more than a traditional place of worship. We aim to develop a vibrant community institution that promotes spiritual development, compassion, social responsibility and unity.

Through our religious services, charitable initiatives and community activities, we seek to bring people together, support families and create a positive environment for present and future generations.

Guided by the values of Islam, we are committed to continuous improvement, responsible management, transparency and serving our community with sincerity and dedication.

Our mission is simple: to strengthen faith, nurture knowledge, unite our community and serve humanity.
